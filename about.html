[01] About

There’s more to a good product
than meets the eye.

I’m a product designer who enjoys looking beyond the interface to understand the people, problems, and decisions that shape a product. My work is driven by a curiosity to understand user behaviour and a desire to create experiences that are intuitive, purposeful, and thoughtful.

Currently, I’m the Founding Product Designer at Optima Learn, where I work across the product lifecycle, from identifying user pain points and shaping product experiences to building scalable design systems and bringing ideas to life. I’ve designed everything from learning journeys and dashboards to AI-powered experiences, working closely with founders and engineers to turn complex requirements into meaningful solutions.

Being part of an early-stage startup has given me the opportunity to think beyond design deliverables and contribute to the product as a whole. I enjoy exploring new ideas, experimenting with AI tools, and finding opportunities to make products better.
